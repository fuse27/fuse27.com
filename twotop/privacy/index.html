TwoTop Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

TwoTop is made by Fuse27 (Adam Bunker, Peoria, Illinois). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it stays on your iPhone, and we never receive it.

What we collect

Nothing. TwoTop has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't run servers that receive your data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The restaurants you save, and your favorites, want-to-try list and never list.
  • Your visit history and settings.
  • Your home location, used to measure distances.

This information is stored only on your device. Deleting TwoTop deletes it.

Location

With your permission, TwoTop uses your location to find nearby restaurants and to tell when you're away from home. Searches go to Apple Maps, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy. TwoTop doesn't share your location with anyone else, and you can turn location access off at any time in Settings.

Other apps

When you tap Website, Directions or a restaurant's details, TwoTop opens Safari, Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze. Those apps follow their own privacy policies.

Beta testing

If you test TwoTop through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with us, under Apple's TestFlight terms. We use them only to fix and improve the app.

Children

TwoTop isn't directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: feedback@fuse27.com
