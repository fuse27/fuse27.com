Questions

Why does TwoTop ask for my location?

To find restaurants near you and to notice when you're on a trip. If you said no, turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › TwoTop. You can still add places by name without it.

A restaurant I know is missing.

Tap + on the Places tab and search for it by name. For a place in another city, add the city to the search, like "EsterEv Milwaukee".

What do Fire it and 86 it do?

Fire it means you're going: TwoTop logs the visit and that place sits out for a while. 86 it skips the place for tonight. Press and hold 86 it to put a place on the never list.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account and nothing is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes your places and history. See the privacy policy.
